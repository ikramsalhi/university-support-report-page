Frequently asked questions

Answers before you need to wait

Search common student questions or browse by topic. If you still need help, send us a request.

Use the university password reset portal with your student ID and recovery method. If you cannot access the recovery option, submit an IT & Digital request and include your student ID.

Confirm your student account is active, forget and reconnect to the campus network, and verify the correct username format. If the problem continues, include your device type and building in the support request.

Enrollment certificates are normally available through the student administrative portal or the registration office. Choose “Registration & Records” when submitting a request if your document is missing.

Submit a Registration & Records request describing the incorrect field. For official identity changes, the university may ask for supporting documentation.

Start with the course instructor or department procedure. If administrative help is required, select Academic Support and include the course name, group, and a concise explanation.

Check your enrollment status first. If registration is confirmed but the course is still missing, send an Academic Support request with the module name and your study program.

Select Finance & Scholarships and include the scholarship name and application reference if available. Do not include bank passwords or other confidential credentials.

Use the Finance & Scholarships category. Include the relevant academic year and payment reference, but never submit card numbers, PINs, or banking passwords.
